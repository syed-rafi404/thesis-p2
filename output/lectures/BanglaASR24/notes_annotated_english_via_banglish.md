# BanglaASR24: Control Theory
The lecture covers the concepts of transient time, steady state, and infinite error in control theory.

## Key takeaways
- Transient time (tp) is the time taken for the system to reach a certain percentage of its final value.
- Overshoot is the maximum amount of time by which the response exceeds the final value.
- Settling time is the time taken for the system to stabilize after the transient phase.
- Understanding transient and steady-state behavior is crucial for designing efficient and reliable systems.
- Infinite error is a theoretical concept indicating that certain levels of error are inevitable in practical applications.
