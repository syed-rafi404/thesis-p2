\section*{Abstract}
Visually impaired and low-vision students often face significant barriers in traditional classroom environments, particularly in comprehending content displayed on boards, such as handwritten notes, diagrams, charts, and other visual elements. This thesis proposes InsightLens, a real-time, vision-language based accessibility framework designed to extract, interpret, and present classroom board content in both Bengali and English. The system leverages computer vision techniques to capture and enhance board visuals, applies optical character recognition (OCR) for text extraction, and utilizes vision-language models to semantically interpret complex elements such as diagrams and graphs. By integrating multilingual processing capabilities, InsightLens can translate and summarize the extracted information to support a diverse range of learners. The framework is designed for deployment on portable or wearable devices, aiming to deliver real-time, context-aware educational assistance. Through this work, we seek to promote inclusive learning environments by bridging the accessibility gap using modern AI technologies, with a specific focus on low-resource languages and bilingual educational context.
