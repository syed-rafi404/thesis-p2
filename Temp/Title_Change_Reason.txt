Request to Revise the Thesis Title

Current title:
InsightLens: A Vision-Language Based Accessibility Framework for Extracting and Understanding Classroom Content in Dual Languages

Proposed title:
InsightLens: A Vision-Language Based Framework for Extracting and Understanding Classroom Content in Dual Languages

Change: the word "Accessibility" is removed. The rest of the title is unchanged.

Reasons for the change

1. The thesis does not evaluate accessibility. Accessibility motivated the project, but no part of the completed work measures an accessibility outcome: there is no study with learners who have hearing or visual impairments, no user or usability study, and no assessment against accessibility guidelines. A title that names an "Accessibility Framework" implies such an evaluation, which a reader or examiner would reasonably expect and not find.

2. Every contribution that was evaluated is still described by the revised title.
   - Vision-language: a vision-language model (Qwen2.5-VL) transcribes the whiteboard, raising the recall of hand-verified board content from 31.2% to 88.8% across 35 boards.
   - Extracting: speech recognition fine-tuned for code-mixed Bengali-English speech lowers the character error rate on unseen lecturers from 72.8% to 50.3%, and the board content above is extracted from the lecture video.
   - Understanding: a language model combines the extracted speech and board content into structured lecture notes, which retain 88.0% of the board content against 37.2% for the original pipeline.
   - Dual languages: all recognition and note generation works on lectures delivered in code-mixed Bengali and English.

3. The change narrows a claim rather than changing the topic. The research questions, methods, data, results and chapter structure are unaffected; only a word describing an outcome the work did not measure is removed.

4. Accessibility remains the motivation of the work and is discussed as an application and as future work, where a study with learners who depend on lecture notes would be the natural next step.
