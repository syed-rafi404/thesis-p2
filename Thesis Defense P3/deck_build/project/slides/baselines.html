What existing models actually produce

Five published models, our own 177 held-out clips. Not one produced romanized Banglish on a single clip. Four write Bengali script, the fifth translates to English.

This is a measured slide, not a related-work list. The orange bars transliterate their Bengali to Roman to be fair to them; the best still sits at 47.1 against our 15.8. If asked: we are not claiming a better Bengali recogniser, we did not test that.
